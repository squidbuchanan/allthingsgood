What we believe

Three things we hold to be true.

01 — Story

Good news is worth telling.

The internet has a bias for outrage. We have a bias for hope. Every post is a small act of resistance against a steady drip of bad news.

02 — Community

The audience is the project.

Five million people don't follow us for memes alone. They follow us because they want to feel the way the world feels when it's at its best. We curate, they amplify.

03 — Impact

Words can lead to action.

As a 501(c)(3), we don't just talk about good — we fund it. We partner with non-profits, spotlight grassroots work, and turn attention into measurable support.

Our story

It started with one simple idea.

In 2019, there was a lot of bad on the internet — and not nearly enough good. So we started sharing the other side: the kid lemonade-stand stories, the strangers helping strangers, the small wins that don't trend but matter.

“The good stuff is happening all around us. We just decided to point a camera at it.”

What began as a quiet feed of feel-good moments has grown into a community of over five million people who show up every month for a daily dose of optimism. We're a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit — every share, every donation, every minute of attention helps fund the work of telling more of these stories.

We don't think good news is naive. We think it's necessary.
